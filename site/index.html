Install

ClipForge is not signed by Microsoft or Apple, so your device shows a warning the first time. That is expected.

Windows

With the installer (recommended)

  1. Open the downloaded ClipForge-Setup.exe.
  2. If you see "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Click through the installer. It needs no administrator password and puts ClipForge in the Start menu.
  4. Open the Start menu and choose ClipForge.

Or with the zip (nothing is installed)

  1. Right-click the downloaded ClipForge-windows.zip and choose Extract All.
  2. Inside is a folder called ClipForge. Move it to where you want to keep it, for example Documents.
  3. Keep the folder named ClipForge, with no version number: ClipForge updates itself inside this folder.
  4. Open the folder and double-click clipforge.exe. If you see "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway.

Do not start ClipForge from a folder whose name ends in .update-new: that is a download waiting to be put in place.

Full guide for Windows (a small page to download and open; the same page is inside the download as "Read me first").

Mac

  1. Double-click the downloaded ClipForge-macos.zip to unzip it. A folder appears with ClipForge in it.
  2. Drag ClipForge into your Applications folder.
  3. macOS 15 or newer: open ClipForge once. macOS says it cannot open it: close that message. Then open System Settings, choose Privacy & Security, scroll down, click Open Anyway next to the line about ClipForge, and confirm.
  4. Older macOS: right-click ClipForge, choose Open, then Open again.

This is needed once, because ClipForge is not signed by Apple. After that it opens like any other app.

Full guide for Mac (a small page to download and open; the same page is inside the download as "Read me first").

Android

  1. Tap the download button on this page, on the phone or tablet itself, and wait for the download to finish.
  2. Open the downloaded file from your notifications or the Files app.
  3. If asked, allow "Install unknown apps" for your browser or file manager, then go back.
  4. Tap Install. If Play Protect asks, choose Install anyway.
  5. Tap Open, or find ClipForge among your apps.

New versions install over the old one and keep your projects. There is no separate guide for Android: these steps are all of it, and the app's Help has the rest.

From version 1.12 on, ClipForge updates itself: when a new version is out, the app shows "Version … is available" with an Update button. You only need this page for the first install, or as the other way if an update does not work. Your projects are kept.

Why ClipForge

  • Simple: the tools you need for short everyday videos.
  • Free. No subscription and no watermark.
  • Works offline. Your videos stay on your device.
  • The same app on a phone and a computer.

What it can do

  • Cut: split, trim, reorder, speed, remove silences, undo with a history list.
  • Picture: wide, phone and square shapes, crop, rotate, a second video in a corner or side by side, filters and fades.
  • Sound: up to 3 sound tracks, clean up sound, music that lowers itself under speech, a separately recorded voice lined up with the picture.
  • Music: your own files or free music search, fitted to the length of the video.
  • Text: titles and captions, with fonts for English, Russian and Hebrew.
  • Voice to text (computer only): captions from speech, and gentle removal of "um" and "uh".
  • Export: full quality, small files for messaging, or sound only.
